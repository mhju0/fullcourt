The trade, honestly. A keeps every installed icon and cached social card stable, and blue/red reads instantly as "two teams" even to someone who has never seen the app — but it speaks a palette the product retired, and the grammar must carry a written exemption forever. B makes the whole identity one sentence — the mark's halves are the data poles you see in every chart — at the cost of a one-time visible change to icons users may already have installed, and rose/teal is a less primal "two sides" read than red/blue. Semantics note: in the app, rose marks the fatigued side, so in B the rested half cools down (teal) and the tired half heats up (rose) — arguably the more truthful drawing.
