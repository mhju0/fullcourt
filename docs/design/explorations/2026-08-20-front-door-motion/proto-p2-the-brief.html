03 · WHAT THE SCORE IS MADE OF

Recent workload

Games in the last 30 days, decaying. Last night counts far more than last week.

Travel

Miles between arenas, log-scaled. No phantom trips home.

Body clock

Time zones crossed, charged harder going east, fading as the team adjusts.

Back-to-backs

Weighted by the real hours between tip-offs, not just the calendar.

Altitude

Denver, Utah and Mexico City — plus the night after.

Density

Games per window against a normal pace, not a raw count.
