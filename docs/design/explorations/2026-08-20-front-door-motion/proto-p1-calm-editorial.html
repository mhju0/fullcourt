What the score is made of

Six measurements of the same night, combined into one number per team. Each is a physical fact about the schedule, not a rating of the roster.

Recent workload

Games in the last 30 days, decaying. Last night counts for far more than last week.

Travel

Miles between arenas, log-scaled. No phantom trips home.

Body clock

Time zones crossed, charged harder going east, fading as the team adjusts.

Back-to-backs

Weighted by the real hours between tip-offs, not just the calendar.

Altitude

Denver, Utah and Mexico City — plus the night after.

Density

Games per window against a normal pace, not a raw count.
