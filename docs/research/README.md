Companion JSON, assignment-correction records, and duplicate-assessment reviews are inputs or
reproducibility evidence, not redundant exports to delete. Producers and protocols live in
[ml/](../../ml/README.md). Raw snapshots are local under `ml/data/l2m-research/`; the refresh
workflow also retains source artifacts for its configured retention period.

Selected late-game reviews cannot establish whole-game accuracy or individual-official blame.
Beneficiary summaries did not pass source-integrity checks, so helped/hurt rankings are not
published. Preserve those limitations when adding seasons.
