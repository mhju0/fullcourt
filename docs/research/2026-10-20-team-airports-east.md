# Eastern Conference team-airport evidence

Research date: **2026-10-20**. Scope: public historical team travel and official airport/FBO capabilities for a proposed 2026-27 travel audit. No live private-flight tracking, exact future flight plans, model changes, or source-data mutations were performed.

## What the evidence permits

The public primary sources found here do **not** establish a current, exclusive “usual charter airport” for all 15 teams. Treat the airport column as a **candidate set**, not a production lookup. A named team departure proves one departure; a private terminal proves a facility exists; neither proves every game itinerary. Home-team departures and visiting-team arrivals also need separate evidence.

Evidence levels:

- **T — team/event primary:** the team or airport authority describes a specific team movement. Strong for that event; current routine remains unverified.
- **C — capability primary:** the airport/FBO describes its own facilities. Supports a candidate, not a team's preference or fleet compatibility on every date.
- **D — directions primary:** team media instructions name an airport. This concerns arena access, not the playing party's charter.
- **N — news lead:** NBA-hosted AP/media reporting or independent reporting. Kept separate from verified primary operational evidence.
- **U — unverified:** no qualifying team-specific operational statement found in this bounded search. This means not established, not disproved.

Unless a publication date is given below, an operational page is undated and was inspected on **2026-10-20**. Source dates and event dates must not be conflated.

## Ground travel and local pairs

1. **Milwaukee → Chicago: one actual bus movement, primary team logistics evidence.** [Clippers behind-the-scenes report, 2015-12-16](https://www.nba.com/clippers/news/behind-scenes-five-game-trip) records a postgame bus between those cities. This proves the route is used by an NBA traveling party, not that every Bulls/Bucks movement uses a bus. Keep date, direction and visiting team with the observation.
2. **Knicks ↔ Nets: shared metro, venue movement separate from aviation.** [NBA staff schedule analysis, 2022](https://api-hub-dev.nba.com/news/schedule-breakdown-bucks-bulls-and-lakers-have-toughest-roads-ahead) treats a Knicks road game in Brooklyn as local. [Team-hosted MTA release, 2015](https://www.nba.com/knicks/limited-edition-metrocards-herald-nba-all-star-nyc) establishes ground access to both venues, not the players' exact mode. Avoid manufacturing a charter between two NYC venue coordinates.
3. **Philadelphia–NYC, Philadelphia–Washington, Miami–Orlando and nearby Midwest pairs:** retain as **mode-uncertain candidates**. No universal bus threshold or team-specific current transport commitment was established. Public intercity services and geographic closeness alone do not select a team's mode. A ground leg still has distance/time; “no flight” must not become “no travel.”

## Return-home finding and an excluded-but-useful lead

**No current primary team/airport source found here establishes a rule such as “return home after N off days” for any Eastern team.** No gap threshold should be calibrated from an absence of reports. Individual-player permission, trade/recruiting flights, holidays and a trip-ending home game are different cases from the whole squad returning home between consecutive away fixtures.

A credible **firsthand independent-reporting lead**, outside the requested official-source acceptance set, is unusually specific: WBEZ Bulls beat reporter Cheryl Raye-Stout wrote on **2012-11-21** that the Bulls would return to Chicago after Houston for Thanksgiving, then finish the trip in Milwaukee on Saturday. Her [2012-11-14 report](https://www.wbez.org/culture/2012/11/14/bulls-vacate-the-united-center-to-make-way-for-circus) includes team-staff interviews and the same planned home interruption; [2012-11-21 report](https://www.wbez.org/culture/2012/11/21/sports-never-take-a-holiday) supplies the Houston→Chicago→Milwaukee sequence. This is evidence of a **reported plan**, not observed flight completion and not a 2026-27 policy. It warrants follow-up, not a silent production override.

For sensitivity work, the defensible distinction is:

- **Direct venue-chain estimate:** known game venues linked in schedule order; actual intermediate whereabouts unknown.
- **Return-via-home scenario:** an explicitly hypothetical additional home stop, preserving the same game venues and dates.
- **Documented itinerary:** event-specific route supported by a dated source. None of the evidence here supplies a complete 2026-27 itinerary.

## Neutral and exceptional venues

- **Orlando is not always the venue for a Magic-designated game.** The NBA's [2025-07-30 official release](https://pr.nba.com/grizzlies-magic-berlin-london-2026-games/) identifies Uber Arena, Berlin (**2026-01-15**) and The O2, London (**2026-01-18**); its [2026-01-29 official recap](https://pr.nba.com/nba-games-berlin-london-records/) confirms the games occurred. Actual game venue must drive the spatial endpoint. This source does not disclose a team airport, hotel or intermediate travel mode.
- The same 2025 release announced **Manchester and Paris for 2027**, while leaving teams/dates for later announcement. That old release is **not enough to map 2026-27 team rows**. Check the current fixture-specific official announcement before assigning neutral endpoints.
- Boston's Logan→London departure is an **international-event exception**, which is exactly why it cannot prove a domestic BOS-only rule.
- Weather can change routes. NBA-hosted AP [2025-06-03 Pacers report](https://www.nba.com/news/severe-weather-delays-pacers-arrival-in-oklahoma-city) describes an Indianapolis charter diversion through Tulsa. **N**, not primary operational proof; a useful reminder that an itinerary estimate is not a flown-track measurement.

## Recommended next evidence to obtain

Keep this airport inventory as a research reference or an explicitly separate airport-based sensitivity proxy. It does not justify replacing every model input. Correct physical game venues and neutral-site fixtures before interpreting smaller differences between metro airports; that prioritization is a modeling recommendation, not a measurement made by this note.

For a team-level airport table, require a recent team travel feature, team-authorized airport/FBO statement, or dated official travel-party departure/arrival naming the airport. Save event date, publication date, home/visitor role, whole-team versus individual scope, neutral venue and uncertainty alongside the airport. Check more than one season before calling a mapping routine. Do not contact teams or service providers without owner authorization.

This research verifies public source content and the evidence distinctions above. It does **not** verify actual 2026-27 aircraft movements, home-return probabilities, flown distance, travel time, airport coordinate accuracy, current charter contracts, or any FullCourt calculation. The parent audit owns source/test reconciliation and whether a separate scenario is useful.
