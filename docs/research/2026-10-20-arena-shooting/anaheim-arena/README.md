# Anaheim Convention Center Arena location

Accepted mapped roof point: **33.80228943029974, -117.91983669078155**. This is the area centroid of the original OpenStreetMap **Arena** building polygon, way **368791749**, version **3**, with 38 vertices including closure. It is not the convention-center campus marker and is not a surveyed court center.

Original source: [OSM way full response](https://api.openstreetmap.org/api/0.6/way/368791749/full), retained as `osm-arena-way-368791749.xml`, SHA256 `ce44cd828c48be70461099a1c97ccdede6f5d5ec1c9b9e9f0b88d30910591eaa`. The exact vertices and derivation are in `arena-coordinate.json`. The centroid uses a local longitude scale and polygon area, with its result replayed by `python3 verify.py`.

Physical identity is corroborated by the [official 2026 specification guide](https://assets.simpleviewinc.com/simpleview/image/upload/v1/clients/anaheimca/ACC_Spec_Guide_2026_singles_701cb92a-9b24-448c-9bf5-e007efa8aec2.pdf), directly linked by the [official Visit Anaheim arena page](https://www.visitanaheim.org/meetings/anaheim-convention-center/floor-plans-and-specifications/arena-capacities/). Page 13 labels the circular Arena south of Katella Avenue, east of the main halls and west of ACC North/Arena Plaza. Page 18 gives the separate Arena floor-plan position. Both pages were rendered and visually inspected against the original OSM circular named roof and surrounding buildings. The PDF and its original response hash are retained locally; see `operator-linked-2026-spec-guide.metadata.json`.

The City of Anaheim's [facility guide](https://www.anaheim.net/DocumentCenter/View/35656/Facility-Guide), [Arena specifications](https://www.anaheim.net/DocumentCenter/View/35651/Arena-Specifications), and [official campus/site map](https://local.anaheim.net/docs_agend/questys_pub/16771/16801/16804/18854/18860/2.%20Exhibit%20A18860.pdf) independently distinguish the Arena from exhibit halls. The web provider could read their text/map descriptions; direct acquisition returned HTTP403 or timeout, so no raw source hash is claimed for those failed downloads. Failures are preserved in `capture-manifest.json`.

The previous candidate **33.80057,-117.92072** is about **208 m southwest** of this roof centroid. A generic Google result named Anaheim Convention Center refers to the campus and was not accepted as arena-specific evidence. Honda Center is a different physical venue.
