# Eastern historical arena research

Research date: 2026-10-20. Scope: all 15 current Eastern franchises and their same-franchise precursors from 1985-86, with regular/postseason game anchors. First dates for pre-existing arenas are the first game in scope, not the building’s first-ever NBA game. Current episodes remain open; the table’s last date is the latest actual fixture in the frozen snapshot. Preseason is excluded.

The machine-readable delivery is `eastern-research-registry.json`: 37 episodes, 35 default/temporary physical sites, plus four secondary sites (Maple Leaf Gardens, Copps, Hartford, Baltimore). Coordinates are named map points, historical polygon centroids, or documented historical map-derived site points. They are not survey or exact court-center measurements. Effective travel-base dates are deliberately null: first home appearance is not evidence that a team moved its base that morning.

## Independent source conflicts and coordinate findings

- Historical NBA game objects contain retroactive wrong venue names: Milwaukee MECCA games become Bradley Center, Detroit Silverdome becomes Palace, Chicago Stadium becomes United Center, Richfield becomes Quicken Loans Arena, Charlotte Coliseum becomes Spectrum Center, and Philadelphia’s two buildings share an arena ID/name. Date/status/team/score verification is independent of these unreliable venue labels.
- Atlanta 1997–99 is interleaved, not a simple one-arena date range: all 73 home fixtures were captured, with 44 Georgia Dome and 29 Alexander/Georgia Tech observations. Use fixture adjudication rather than assigning either building to the whole interval. [Georgia Tech primary guide](https://repository.gatech.edu/bitstreams/6a55bc7f-ff78-4c13-80ae-e0cc949f2e9e/download) corroborates the Hawks tenancy. [Current operator](https://ramblinwreck.com/facilities/mccamish-pavilion) confirms Alexander/McCamish retained the same footprint/roof structure through renovation.
- Toronto pre-ACC was exhaustively captured: 126 fixtures, 117 SkyDome, six Maple Leaf Gardens, two Copps, one provider `tbd`; that last fixture is independently adjudicated as Copps by the retained AFP photographer caption in `../alternate-fixtures/adjudications.json`. The 1996-11-01 Knicks fixture is SkyDome in the authentic NBA game record despite the 2017 Knicks guide listing Maple Leaf Gardens. No additional Gardens override was inferred from that guide.
- Richfield: original USGS 1994 Peninsula roof-symbol centroid **41.24854740747868, -81.59458846012393**. The native GeoNames point is about 14 m from the roof centroid; the previously proposed southern point is roughly 360 m away and was rejected. [USGS original map](https://prd-tnm.s3.amazonaws.com/StagedProducts/Maps/HistoricalTopo/PDF/OH/24000/OH_Peninsula_228030_1994_24000_geo.pdf), [NPS original site history](https://home.nps.gov/cuva/learn/historyculture/former-coliseum-property.htm) and retained named historical aerial corroborate the former physical site.
- Miami Arena: original USGS 1994 map explicitly labels the building. Derived roof-symbol centroid **25.781168086529952, -80.19473297469061**; the northern comparison candidate was outside the arena. [USGS map](https://prd-tnm.s3.amazonaws.com/StagedProducts/Maps/HistoricalTopo/PDF/FL/24000/FL_Miami_347456_1994_24000_geo.pdf), [original arena developer](https://www.barkerpacific.com/commercial-projects/miami-arena) identifies 721 NW 1st Avenue.
- Orlando old arena: original 1995 Orlando West map roof rectangle centroid **28.549001355481256, -81.38657097682642**. The eastern comparison candidate was outside that roof. [USGS map](https://prd-tnm.s3.amazonaws.com/StagedProducts/Maps/HistoricalTopo/PDF/FL/24000/FL_Orlando%20West_347871_1995_24000_geo.pdf), [municipal former-site record](https://orlando.novusagenda.com/AgendaPublic/AttachmentViewer.ashx?AttachmentID=70311&ItemID=41659) corroborates the historical parcel.
- The USGS derivations retain every manually traced image vertex, PDF crop/scale, original GeoPDF CTM/projection, exact ArcGIS transformation request/response, raw hashes, and four neatline controls. The controls reproduce the geographic quadrangle corners with expected datum shifts. Coordinates represent printed map symbols at 1:24000, with practical accuracy in tens of meters.
- Market Square uses the parent-approved inferred site anchor **39.768375, -86.15171935**, midpoint of original OSM Alabama–Market and New Jersey–Market intersections. [Original municipal map](https://gis.indy.gov/portal/sharing/rest/content/items/3d723f5fa3974a68856d5848b395380c/data) identifies the historic arena site. It is a venue-scale proxy, not a measured old roof/court center.
- Original OHM building polygons independently supply Charlotte Coliseum (Tyvola), Silverdome, Palace, and Bradley Center centroids. Provider tags and complete vertices are retained in `ohm-derived-historical-centroids.json`. Historical tags do not establish first/last NBA game dates.

## Secondary fixtures and open boundaries

## Reproduction and evidence

Run from this directory: `python3 build_inventory.py`, `python3 check_sources.py`, `python3 build_delivery.py`. The source collector and retained metadata preserve raw response bytes and SHA256; immutable raw sources remain in this local research tree for the parent to archive under ignored audit storage. Source URLs and hashes are also in each coordinate record. No application files or database rows were modified.

The independent fixture check validates exact external ID, Eastern date, final status, both team identities after documented historical aliases, and both scores against the frozen source snapshot. It deliberately does not certify provider arena labels. See `fixture-identity-check.json` for exact counts and failures.

Remaining verification limits: the Omni, Boston Garden, Chicago Stadium, old Spectrum and Capital Centre GeoNames points are original named venue records, not independently traced roof footprints; standing venue map pins may be entrances rather than centers. Every point retains its actual method and precision boundary. This inventory is evidence for the root’s semantic review, not a blanket certification of every historical venue assignment.
