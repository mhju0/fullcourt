## Direct evidence

**Las Vegas, 0049100030.** [Mark Heisler's May 4, 1992 staff match report](https://www.latimes.com/archives/la-xpm-1992-05-04-sp-907-story.html)
names “Nevada Las Vegas’ Thomas & Mack Center.” The root's separately retained
[Lakers history](https://www.nba.com/lakers/news/161014-lakers-history-las-vegas)
corroborates the relocation's date and city. In that team history, the other
Thomas & Mack mentions concern different events; the contemporary match report
provides the direct building evidence for this fixture.

**March 1991 Tacoma, 0029000930.** The [March 28, 1991 Big Spring Herald scan](https://newspapers.swco.ttu.edu/bitstreams/841e9688-25b5-4014-b129-1cc03f9a0be7/download),
PDF page 12, printed page 2-B, carries AP's match report. Its Seattle paragraph
says “playing the second of three games at the Tacoma Dome.” The report's
Portland 112–Seattle 107 result identifies the fixture. The dated source is an
actual match report, not a later whole-season venue summary.

**April 1991 Tacoma, 0029001023.** [AP's April 11, 1991 report](https://www.latimes.com/archives/la-xpm-1991-04-11-sp-38-story.html)
places the game “Wednesday night at the Tacoma Dome.” This is the requested
April 10 fixture, rather than the later 1994-95 season's full Tacoma residency.

**Baltimore, 0029200320.** The team-authored [2014-15 Wizards media guide, library mirror](https://library.sfo2.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/publications/basketball/yearbooks/KWASBMG-2015.pdf),
PDF page 165 / printed page 176, lists the 1992-93 fixture as
“Dec.26 Detroit (B) 97-99”; its legend reads “(B) Baltimore Arena.”
This explicitly ties the date, opponent, score and arena. A
[Mitchell Layton/Getty photo caption](https://www.gettyimages.co.nz/detail/news-photo/dennis-rodman-of-the-detroit-pistons-dribbles-up-court-news-photo/1291717294)
instead labels the same date Capital Centre/Landover. Preserve that conflict;
the primary team log is the positive fixture-specific Baltimore evidence. The
guide's other errors below prohibit treating every field in it as authoritative.

**Anaheim, 0029300536.** The team-authored [2015-16 Clippers media guide, library mirror](https://library.sfo2.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/publications/basketball/yearbooks/KLACLMG-2016.pdf),
PDF page 138, explicitly dates its first regular-season Pond game against the
Knicks to January 27, 1994. It explains the Northridge earthquake relocation.
The previous timeline entry identifies the building as Arrowhead Pond of Anaheim.
The history incorrectly prints a 101–103 score; retain that source error and
preserve the actual canonical 102–103 result. The venue assertion does not depend
on accepting the erroneous score.

**Hamilton, 0029600385.** [Carlo Allegri's AFP photo metadata, Getty editorial 52011579](https://www.gettyimages.com.mx/detail/fotograf%C3%ADa-de-noticias/toronto-raptor-marcus-camby-tries-to-block-new-fotograf%C3%ADa-de-noticias/52011579)
places Nets–Raptors “at Copps Coliseum in Hamilton.” The creation date is December
26, 1996; the caption gives the 98–96 Toronto result and the object identifier
`SAPA961227081560`. This is firsthand agency reporting. The separate
[AP match roundup dated December 27](https://www.spokesman.com/stories/1996/dec/27/kidd-a-goat-in-dallas-sent-to-suns/)
independently confirms the previous day's fixture and Hamilton location. No
licensed photograph is copied into the published research note.

## Triangulated building placement

**November 1990 Tacoma, 0029000062.** [Dan Hafner's November 11, 1990 postgame roundup](https://www.latimes.com/archives/la-xpm-1990-11-11-sp-6134-story.html)
explicitly describes “playing a home game at Tacoma.” This independently
establishes the city for the requested fixture. Its text does not name the Dome.
The March 1991 AP record identifies that March fixture as the second of three
Tacoma Dome games. The April AP report independently places the April fixture
at the Dome; the November report places the earlier fixture in Tacoma. The
secondary season log also identifies those three dates as the Dome fixtures.
Together these support a high-confidence derived November Dome placement,
but do not provide a direct fixture-specific primary building assertion.
Keep this distinction in any acceptance record; do not call the NBA's KeyArena
label correct simply because the stronger arena proof remains incomplete.

## Wider inventory and retained conflicts

- The Wizards guide has 34 explicit `(B)` game markers from 1989-01-06 through
  1997-03-29. None appears on the 1985-86, 1986-87 or 1987-88 season pages. This
  bounded guide check finds no new pre-1988 Baltimore fixture; it is not proof
  that every possible second-city fixture has been enumerated.
- The guide omits a Baltimore marker on the separately NBA-confirmed
  1992-01-18 PHI at WAS fixture. It also dates Cleveland's 105–101 loss in
  Baltimore as March 13, 1993, whereas the fresh canonical/NBA identity is March
  15. Do not change either canonical date from this guide. The existing
  1997-02-14 NJN at WAS entry explains one guide fixture outside the 34 new
  candidates. The private inventory preserves all literal guide rows.
- A new report-only conflict emerged: [UPI's March 30, 1991 roundup](https://www.upi.com/Archives/1991/03/30/NBA-Roundup/9058670309200/)
  places the preceding MIN at SEA 117–107 game in Tacoma. The March 30
  [Midland Reporter-Telegram scan](https://newspapers.swco.ttu.edu/server/api/core/bitstreams/d8113179-0bd1-4808-800e-2dc5b44f8844/content),
  PDF page 12 / printed 2B, carries AP's NBA roundup with the exact result under
  a `SEATTLE` dateline. The AP byline and fixture paragraph were visually checked
  on that page. [Dan Hafner's separate March 30 report](https://www.latimes.com/archives/la-xpm-1991-03-30-sp-939-story.html)
  also says “at Seattle.” Reject a Tacoma correction based on UPI's contrary
  city alone; retain the disagreement. No direct building assertion was found
  for this extra fixture. This bounded resolution does not establish that the
  secondary 101-candidate list is exhaustive.
