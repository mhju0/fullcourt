# Eastern-team itinerary evidence: home interruptions versus staying away

Researched **2026-10-20**. This deepens the [airport inventory](2026-10-20-team-airports-east.md); it makes no application, database or model changes. “Home interruption” means a team returns to its home area **between two away games without an intervening home game**. An individual player's injury, family visit or trade itinerary is a separate observation.

## Findings

There are documented whole-team home interruptions. The strongest Eastern examples found are **Bulls, November 2012; Nets, March 2016; Sixers, December 2024**. They support researching geography, practice and holidays together. They do not provide frequency data, a universal time threshold, or proof of the particular Knicks Boston→New York→Los Angeles hypothesis.

The opposite behavior is also documented: teams practice or stay in an away city before the next away game. A three-calendar-day game gap can accommodate either behavior. Evidence should choose an event's route when available; otherwise both routes remain estimates.

## Evidence tiers

- **T:** official team report with actual team logistics or firsthand travel-coordinator testimony.
- **J:** named beat/agency reporter's contemporary firsthand reporting, especially observed practice/departure or a coach's account. Journalism is primary evidence for reported events, not an official permanent team policy.
- **P:** a reported future plan. Keep separate from observed completion.
- **L:** secondary aggregation or search-index excerpt whose original body was not fully available; follow-up lead, not a fully accepted case.
- **H:** research hypothesis. No observed itinerary is claimed.

Sources were inspected on 2026-10-20 through original pages and search-index excerpts. Basketball-Reference direct opens returned HTTP403; indexed game-log content supplied calendar corroboration where stated. Dates below distinguish the event from publication.

## Accepted home-interruption cases

### CHI-2012-11: Houston → Chicago area → Milwaukee

**Events:** at Houston **2012-11-21**; Chicago-area practice **2012-11-23**; at Milwaukee **2012-11-24**. **Whole team**, with normal limitations that a practice report is not an attendance census for every roster member.

**J, actual corroboration:** Mike McGraw's [Daily Herald report, published 2012-11-23, 12:19 p.m.](https://www.dailyherald.com/20121123/pro-sports/bulls-look-for-right-mix-at-point-guard/) places the Friday activity at the **Berto Center**, with Hinrich and Thibodeau interviews, following Wednesday's Houston loss and before Saturday's Milwaukee game. His [companion report, published 2012-11-23, 12:20 p.m.](https://www.dailyherald.com/20121123/pro-sports/bulls-butler-has-done-a-good-job/) reports the team boarding a **coach bus to Milwaukee after practice**.

**P, reason/context corroboration:** Cheryl Raye-Stout's [WBEZ report, published 2012-11-21](https://www.wbez.org/culture/2012/11/21/sports-never-take-a-holiday) describes the planned Thanksgiving return after Houston, before Milwaukee. Her [2012-11-14 report](https://www.wbez.org/culture/2012/11/14/bulls-vacate-the-united-center-to-make-way-for-circus) includes team-staff interviews and the same planned interruption of the circus trip.

**Home-game exclusion:** the [season game log](https://www.basketball-reference.com/teams/CHI/2013.html) lists Houston Nov21 and Milwaukee Nov24 consecutively; the next home game was Milwaukee **Nov26**, after this case. Official NBA event endpoints: [Houston Nov21](https://www.nba.com/rockets/game/0021200165-bulls-vs-rockets-houston-tx-11-21-2012) and [Milwaukee Nov24](https://www.nba.com/game/chi-vs-mil-0021200188).

**Proved:** home-area team practice and subsequent ground departure during consecutive away fixtures. **Not proved:** exact Houston→Chicago landing time, every player's Thanksgiving whereabouts, or a standing Bulls policy. This case directly contradicts assuming that an arena's circus occupation prevents a team returning to its home area or using a separate practice facility.

### BKN-2016-03: Minnesota → Brooklyn home respite → Toronto

**J, reported completed event:** **2016-03-05 Minnesota → Brooklyn respite → 2016-03-08 Toronto; coach Tony Brown confirmed sleeping home; no intervening game.** [AP report, NBC publication 2016-03-08](https://www.nbcsports.com/nba/news/derozan-leads-raptors-past-nets-104-99).

The report concerns the team's road swing, rather than an individual injury exception. Exact arrival/departure dates and a complete roster manifest are absent. The home stop is meaningful evidence against reconstructing every consecutive away fixture as a direct city-to-city transfer.

**Calendar exclusion:** the AP report explicitly excludes games in between. The [ESPN event page](https://www.espn.com/nba/game/_/gameId/400828831/nets-raptors) independently labels Brooklyn as the visitor on March8. Do not infer that the first seven games of a nominal nine-game road trip imply nine uninterrupted nights away.

### PHI-2024-12: Boston → Philadelphia → Salt Lake City

**J, retrospective actual itinerary:** Keith Pompey's [Inquirer report, published 2025-01-05, 9:17 a.m. ET](https://www.inquirer.com/sixers/sixers-finish-4-2-on-road-trip-20250105.html) states the Sixers returned home after the **2024-12-25 Boston game**, had **Dec26 off**, and traveled to **Salt Lake City Dec27**. This is a whole-team trip account, without a per-player manifest.

**Home-game exclusion:** the [2024-25 game log](https://www.basketball-reference.com/teams/PHI/2025_games.html) lists Boston **Dec25** followed by Utah **Dec28**. The [NBA Boston event recap](https://www.nba.com/game/phi-vs-bos-0022400407) calls Utah the second game of the same road trip. There was no intervening home fixture.

**Proved:** a Northeast home interruption before a Western destination, on a two-full-off-day interval. **Not proved:** a home practice, a causal preference for a particular airport, or what the team would have done without Christmas. This is a close structural analogue to the proposed Knicks Boston→NYC→LA route, not proof of that Knicks route.

| **Regional road continuation, visiting Clippers, 2015-12-09 to 12-11** | Rowan Kavner, [team logistics feature, 2015-12-16](https://www.nba.com/clippers/news/behind-scenes-five-game-trip) | **T:** Milwaukee→Chicago bus; overnight Dec10, NYC flight Dec11. | Whole traveling-party account linked to the equipment manager. The Sixers cases are useful controls: **Dec25→Dec28 in 2024 returned home; Dec26→Dec29 in 2008 reportedly stayed away.** The game-date gap is the same. Geography, holiday context, practice and era differ. Neither case alone identifies the cause; together they undermine a deterministic off-day-only rule.

## Knicks Boston → New York → Los Angeles hypothesis

**H — not established as an observed Knicks itinerary in this bounded search.** Searches covered combinations of Knicks/Boston/Los Angeles/West Coast/Greenburgh with return-home, flew-home and practice wording, alongside team reports and named beat reporting. Results frequently referred to an intervening MSG game, an individual player's medical/personal travel, or the end of a trip. Those are not accepted substitutes.

The 2024 Sixers case shows that a Northeast team can return home after Boston before heading West. It raises the hypothesis's plausibility, but the Knicks may choose earlier Western arrival, acclimation or team practice elsewhere. Minimal extra map distance also does not imply a cost-free home stop: another departure, ground transfers and reduced recovery time may matter.

**Best estimate for an unsourced future occurrence:** keep the chosen route labeled *inferred*. If timing allows meaningful home recovery and the home area lies near the onward path, prioritize the home-stop scenario for manual research. Without a dated team/beat source, preserve a direct-route alternative and disclose its effect on the estimate. Do not call the inference verified or assign a numerical probability from these few examples.

**Chicago → Milwaukee:** proximity does not identify where the team sleeps. The 2012 case proves home practice plus a bus, while the Clippers case proves an away-party ground continuation. For Bulls, home-to-Milwaukee is also different from a visiting club's Chicago-to-Milwaukee sequence. Research home-area return, hotel stay and travel mode separately.

## Exclusions that prevent false positives

- **OG Anunoby, March2024:** an [NBC report, 2024-03-19](https://www.nbcsports.com/nba/news/knicks-og-anunoby-likely-out-for-rest-of-road-trip-maybe-longer-with-elbow-flare-up) describes his medical return while teammates continued West. Individual exception; exclude from team itinerary.
- **Stephon Marbury, November2007:** [ESPN, 2007-12-22](https://www.espn.in/nba/news/story?id=3165844) concerns leaving/rejoining the team. Individual disciplinary/personal itinerary; exclude.
- “Returned home to face…” and “home between road trips” often contain a real home game. Verify full fixture sequence before accepting an A→home→B example.
- A nominal “five-game road trip” can contain a home interruption; the label does not establish lodging continuity.
- Stadium occupation, home practice access and players' residences are different facts. Check a separate practice facility before treating arena occupation as a return-home prohibition.

## Proposed research policy for a full-season best estimate

This is a proposed audit procedure, not an implemented algorithm or fitted behavioral model.

1. **Start with authentic fixtures and physical venues.** Confirm ET game dates, actual tip times, neutral/alternate venues and reschedules. Home/away designation is not a coordinate.
2. **Enumerate consecutive-away gaps.** Retain all intervals; use available hours and plausible recovery/practice windows, not a universal N-day trigger. Back-to-backs, early tips and long border transfers can constrain possibilities.
3. **Compare geography explicitly.** For previous venue A, home base H, next venue B, calculate direct and via-home distance/time scenarios and the extra leg. Home along an onward path, a distant detour, and two games within one metro are different research priorities.
4. **Seek a dated movement or practice observation.** Search team communications and named beat reporting around the gap. Prefer actual home/away practice, overnight location, departure or a coordinator's retrospective over schedule-preview language. Keep planned and completed distinct.
5. **Add context without making it deterministic.** Holiday/family opportunity, home practice availability, arena occupation, road-practice access, cross-border formalities, weather, Cup progression and timezone acclimation are candidate explanations. A coach's stated rationale outranks an inferred one.
6. **Record each decision's provenance.** Team-season, surrounding fixtures, intervening-home-game check, event/publication dates, whole-team versus subset, source tier, observed/planned/inferred state, home-stop window and unresolved alternatives.
7. **Choose an estimate with its alternatives visible.** Apply documented routes case by case. For unknowns, publish the selected inferred scenario and sensitivity to the alternative. Do not backfill confidence from a green calculation or conceal an uncertain leg behind an exact mile total.

These cases are examples selected by search, **not a representative sample**. No team-level “usually,” return-home percentage, validated classifier, or complete 2026-27 itinerary is established. The file supplies accepted observations, a qualified counterexample and a concrete research procedure; the root audit owns calculation and product decisions.
