# Western Conference team-airport evidence — 2026-10-20

Research reference for the 2026-27 travel audit. Checked 2026-10-20 against public
team, NBA, airport and FBO pages. No live flight tracking, flight identifiers,
future private itineraries, model inputs, coefficients or database changes.

## Interpretation

- **A — recurring team practice:** an official source expressly describes habitual use.
- **B — observed historical team movement:** a dated team flight or arrival; proves that event, not an exclusive or current preference.
- **C — indirect team association:** a team travel reference, directions or promotion that does not establish regular charter operations.
- **D — airport capability only:** an airport/FBO describes private aviation services; team usage remains unverified.
- **U — unresolved:** no sufficient primary team-airport evidence found in this bounded search.

Every row has a candidate, but a candidate is not a verified usual charter airport.
Historical practice does not establish a 2026-27 operating contract. Proximity,
sponsorship, team ownership of an aircraft, and terminal branding are insufficient
to select a precise airport. FBO names identify terminals/services within airports,
not separate airports. All undated capability pages below were accessed 2026-10-20.

## Consecutive away games and returns home

The bounded primary-source search did **not** establish a league-wide number of
days off after which teams return home, nor a per-team 2026-27 policy. An absence
of a home game does not establish continuous residence on the road. Conversely,
an off day does not establish a homeward flight.

For the other Western teams, no sufficiently explicit primary evidence of
whole-team home returns **between consecutive away games** was established here.
Standard end-of-trip returns, solo player travel, home games between two trips,
emergency turnbacks, and local marketing appearances were not counted as proof.

## Audit implications and open verification

1. Keep this inventory as research provenance or a separate airport-distance
   sensitivity analysis. Do not silently replace arena-based model inputs.
2. Only SAC/SMF has explicit recurring-practice wording in the inspected sources.
   MEM, OKC, PHX, PDX, SAT and SLC have event-level team movement evidence; age
   and exceptions remain material. Other rows remain indirect/capability candidates.
3. Resolve **actual venue coordinates**, including Austin/neutral/international
   sites, before refining airports. A correct airport cannot repair a wrong arena.
4. If modeling home returns, make itinerary uncertainty explicit: direct
   arena-to-arena baseline versus a clearly named hypothetical via-home scenario.
   No automatic two/three/four-day return threshold was verified.
5. The [Signature HOU page](https://www.signatureaviation.com/locations/HOU)
   currently lists runway work from 2026-07-13 to 2027-01-29. This is an operational
   caveat, not proof that Rockets flights use HOU or that they will switch to IAH.
