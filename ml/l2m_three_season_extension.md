Apply `l2m_season_preregistration.md` unchanged to all reports linked from each additional NBA season index. Keep snapshots separate. Calculate the same grades, games, represented periods, call categories, monthly summaries, assignment exposures and beneficiary-field audits. Compare regular seasons separately from play-in and playoffs: raw counts, errors per reviewed game/period, proportion of errors graded INC, and category composition. These are descriptive comparisons of indexed L2M samples, not an estimate of full-game accuracy or a causal claim about officials or league directives. Use the existing secondary season foul/technical series for context; no new player or named-referee hypothesis tests.
